# JSW-Energy-Financial-Performance-Stock-Prediction-Analysis
The overview of this project stands on the objective which is to analyze different Financial statements and Stock market data of JSW Energy for the review insights.

The Scope of project aims to analyze current one-year performance of stock of JSW Energy and to give many financial calculation, analysis and performance evaluation between year 2020 to 2024 period in various financial statements i.e. Balance sheet, Income Statement and Cashflow Statement with the help of the analysis and visualization tools like Excel and Power BI.

The outcome of project focuses to give the best beneficiary requirements of cleaned and analyzed data for investors and traders for the future investment, establishment, or further decisions.
The purpose of making this business requirements document is to simplify the analysis and findings in a report document form for better understanding.
The audience for this business requirements document focuses on the investors, stakeholders, traders and financial & analytical authorities.
